## How to use this guide

- Read a question, answer it aloud, then compare your answer. For each topic prepare a real example from **your own** work.
- Each answer includes a small example. Snippets illustrate a concept and may omit setup, imports, error handling, or surrounding types; they are not complete production applications. Behavioral examples are fictional templates, not claims about your experience.
- For a deeper understanding, read the follow-up questions immediately after each related concept.
- For coding, write the solution without looking, test the examples and edge cases, and explain time and space complexity.
- Prioritize files 02 through 06 for this job. Use file 08 for mock interviews.
- These questions are **paraphrased practice questions**, not an official Chevron question bank. They combine public interview-report themes, common industry topics, and the supplied job description. Candidate reports are anecdotal, can be old, and may refer to a different role or location. No specific question is guaranteed.
- A Chevron Developer Glassdoor search result reports themes including explaining a project and its business value, fit, weakness, and leadership. The relevant report could not be opened directly during research; its search snippet is a lead, not independent confirmation. An AmbitionBox Chevron Holdings page contains mixed or potentially unrelated content, so we do not attribute its questions to this role.
- Technical answers are study guidance, not a substitute for checking current Microsoft documentation and your organization's architecture and security requirements.
